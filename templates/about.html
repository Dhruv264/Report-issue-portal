"Our commitment is to create a transparent, accountable, and responsive administration. The Amdavad Citizen Portal is not just a platform; it is a promise of better governance for every resident."

Ahmedabad is a city that beautifully blends its UNESCO World Heritage status with modern aspirations. As we move towards a 'Digital Amdavad', we invite citizens to actively engage with us.

This portal is designed to put power back into your hands, allowing you to report grievances, track resolutions, and access municipal services from the comfort of your home. Together, we can build the city we envision.
